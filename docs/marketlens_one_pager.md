# MarketLens

## See the Market Clearly

**AI-assisted market intelligence for structured, faster market research.**

MarketLens is an AI-powered market research system designed to help businesses, founders, and analysts explore markets from multiple perspectives and turn complex information into structured insights.

---

## The Problem

Understanding a market often means collecting information from many different places and answering many different questions.

You may need to understand:

* Market size and growth
* Competitors and positioning
* Industry trends
* Opportunities
* Risks and challenges
* Technology and skill shifts

The challenge isn't simply finding information.

**The challenge is turning scattered information into a structured view of the market.**

---

## The MarketLens Approach

MarketLens uses a multi-agent research workflow to break a complex market question into specialized research tasks.

Instead of asking a single AI system to do everything, different agents can focus on different aspects of the research.

```text
                Market Question
                       │
                       ▼
                 Planner Agent
                       │
          ┌────────────┴────────────┐
          ▼                         ▼
   Market Research Agent       Risk Research Agent
          │                         │
          └────────────┬────────────┘
                       ▼
                Structured Evidence
                       │
                       ▼
                 Final Report
```

The goal is to make market research more structured, traceable, and reusable.

## What You Receive

A MarketLens analysis can produce:

### 📊 Structured Market Evidence

Machine-readable JSON output that can be validated, processed, and reused.

### 📄 Market Intelligence Report

A human-readable PDF containing the main findings, competitive landscape, risks, and strategic takeaways.

### 🔎 Research Breakdown

A structured view of the different dimensions considered during the analysis.
